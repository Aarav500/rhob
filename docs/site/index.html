Read the threat model before citing any number on this page. Hacking policies in RHOB are scripted, not emergent; the L0-at-chance result is a negative control, not a finding; and every leaderboard cell is a single unreplicated 5-vs-5 draw whose standard error near AUROC 0.5 is 0.19 (the Mann–Whitney null, √((n+m+1)/12nm) at n=m=5). The board has since been replicated over 20 independent draws with bootstrap confidence intervals; the leaderboard now serves those.

A 2026-08 audit corrected several figures previously published here — including an L2 held-out transfer number of 0.95 and an L3 “oracle ceiling” of 0.87 that were both wrong — and then found that the fix to the admission gate had reproduced the original error one level down, certifying 15 cells whose proxy criteria were never actually measurable. The admitted-cell count fell as a result. See the changelog.

The core idea

Real reward hacking means: the proxy reward rises while the true objective collapses. RHOB operationalizes this with matched-proxy pairs — a hacking and a legitimate variant whose proxy reward is statistically indistinguishable while their ground-truth outcomes diverge sharply. Any detector that discriminates between them must read information beyond the proxy.

The reusable contribution is the admission gate: six automated criteria that certify the construction rather than assume it. proxy_matched is a TOST equivalence test (margin ±0.10 on AUROC, two one-sided tests at α=0.05, cluster-bootstrapped over 12 independent layouts) — it demands evidence for a matched proxy instead of merely failing to find evidence against one. That costs 576 rollouts per (family, difficulty) cell, up from 96.

Each criterion returns one of three outcomes, not two: PASS (measured, and matched), FAIL (measured, and leaking), or DEGENERATE (not measurable — the proxy carries no information for the statistic to read). The third case is why the admitted count on this page went down rather than up: an AUROC over a constant proxy is 0.5 by the tie convention rather than by matching, the equivalence interval collapses to a point, and the test then certifies against any margin at all. That is the same "a check nobody can fail" defect the equivalence test was introduced to remove, recurring inside the fix for it. The gate now checks both that the statistic could have come out differently and that the proxy carries signal above numerical dust (relative SD above 1e-4 — a constant plus jitter in its eighth significant digit does not count), and reports such cells as unmeasured instead of passing. Six of the 33 registered families are in that state, covering 30 of the 123 (family, difficulty) cells — and the two questions catch three each, so neither is redundant. physics_exploitation is the case that proves it: a relative SD of 3.3e-4, comfortably above the 1e-4 floor, and an L0 statistic that strictly orders 0.35% of cross-variant pairs. (Earlier versions of this page called the relative-SD distribution "bimodal with nothing between 1.6e-9 and 5.1e-2". That was false — physics_exploitation sits inside that interval, as does goal_misgeneralization at 9.9e-3–1.3e-2. The real structure is a single enormous gap between three families constant to one ULP and everything else, with the floor inside it.)

L0 at chance is a negative control

L0 detectors sit at chance by construction — the families are built so the proxy carries no signal, and where the gate can measure it, the gate certifies that they succeeded. This is a manipulation check confirming the matched-proxy construction, not a discovery that reward-only monitoring fails. The useful direction is the failure one: L0 above chance means the construction leaked and every other number on that family is suspect.

So the check is published rather than asserted. The admission ledger records every criterion's outcome and its measurements for every cell in scope, failures and unmeasurable cells alike. Of the 50 cells currently in scope: 15 ADMITTED, 30 DEGENERATE, 5 NOT ADMITTED.

Read all three numbers together. The matched-proxy property is established on 15 cells, refuted on 5, and unavailable on 30 — every difficulty tier of six families whose proxy no reward-only detector can read: distributional_shift, orbit_chirality and rlhf_reward_model_overopt, whose proxies are constant to within numerical dust, plus monitored_sandbagging, shortcut_exploitation and physics_exploitation, whose proxies vary but come out identical in every run. Degenerate cells are excluded from the L0-at-chance control: on a proxy like that, reward-only detectors sit at chance for a reason that has nothing to do with matching. With all six held out the L0 suite mean is 0.4866 over 27 families, against 0.4895 with them in (both family-weighted; the 0.4898 published in earlier releases is the same statistic un-excluded and weighted by cell — of the 0.0032 difference the exclusion accounts for 0.0029 and the re-weighting for 0.0003). The exclusion applies to L0 and only L0, since the other rungs read channels these families measure perfectly well. They remain valid hacking families — they separate at L2 and their true rewards diverge — and rejoin the control when their proxies are made informative-but-matched. Scope limit: the ledger covers 10 of 33 families; the other 23 are uncertified, which is not the same as certified-and-passing. What runs in CI per family is a reduced-power smoke screen at margin ±0.256, not certification at ±0.10 — and 12 of the 33 families do not run even that (most have hand-written per-family tests instead, which check whatever their author thought to check rather than the gate's six criteria). No test anywhere in tests/ calls AdmissionGate.certify_all_tiers on a registered family — scripts/admission_ledger.py is its only caller.

What this does not show. All 14 train and test families were written by the same authors under the same construction recipe, sharing one behavioral-trace convention, so this measures transfer within a recipe — not generalization to mechanisms nobody designed. The reported spread on the neural detectors is across 5 weight initializations only; the environment draw is unreplicated. L0/L1 transfer at chance is the negative control again, not a finding.

Quick start

pip install rhob  # or: git clone + pip install -e ".[dev]"

from rhob.v3.benchmark import Benchmark
from rhob.detectors import RewardThresholdDetector

detector = RewardThresholdDetector()
results = Benchmark.evaluate(detector, families=["gridworld_camping"], n_seeds=10)
print(results.overall_auroc)

External baselines

RHOB ships classical, citable methods from the wider change-point / anomaly-detection literature so that detection approaches can be compared, not just in-house features: Page-Hinkley change-point test, Isolation Forest anomaly detection, autoregressive sequence-model residuals, PCA reconstruction error, and Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection.

These five are implemented but are not present in the committed leaderboard artifact, and no scores for them are published yet.
