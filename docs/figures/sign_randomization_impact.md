# L2 sign randomization: measured before/after

Generated by `scripts/measure_sign_randomization.py`; numbers come from
`docs/figures/sign_randomization_impact.json`, which also carries every per-cell
AUROC (`cells`). Every figure here is a measurement, not an estimate; anything that
was not measured is named as such.

## Headline

- **RTS (Ensemble Top-5) 0.993 -> 0.514.** With randomization off it reads 0.993, against the published 0.994 (-0.001): the pre-randomization figure does not reproduce exactly.
- **L2 suite mean 0.7320 -> 0.5739; best 0.9807 (Behavioral Threshold) -> 0.9610 (Trajectory MLP).**
- **The L2 detectors that read the feature's *sign* (Behavioral Threshold, Centroid Tracker) scored 0.97-0.98 before and 0.36-0.48 after.** The 4 that read only a magnitude (Angular Momentum, Feature Magnitude, Feature Consistency, Reward-Feature Correlation) scored 0.53-0.59 before and are identical after on every cell scored in both modes.
- **Difficulty knob.** `|AUROC - 0.5|` is invariant under the flip, so randomization cannot revive a pinned knob: 27 of 32 multi-tier families have a separation span of exactly 0.000.

## Design

- `n_seeds` = 10 per variant per cell, layout seed 0, one draw (`n_replicates = 1`).
- Families measured: **33 of 33**.
- Measured at commit `54cd63cfac06` (clean tree), Python 3.12.16.

| `after` | 7 | 0.5739 | 0.1731 | 0.9610 | Trajectory MLP |

SD is the spread across the detectors at the level, not a confidence interval: the detectors share one evaluation draw, so these are not independent replicates. The detectors are not all averaged over the same cells: a detector that declares `min_episodes` is not applicable on families shorter than that, and the `cells scored` column below says how many each one measured. `Trajectory MLP` is fitted inside each cell under 5-fold cross-validation -- its training folds carry labels, so it learns the cell's direction from them. That is legitimate under the benchmark's rules and is why it barely moves, but where it is the `after` maximum, that maximum is a supervised result, not a statement that the unsupervised behavioral signal still separates.

## Cross-family transfer (RTS)

Sharing a seed is the design, not a measurement: whether a trial reproduces there is checked against that file, not assumed here (the +/- across model inits is reported in the JSON, not here).

The L0 and L1 rows are measured in `before` only, and marked `—` elsewhere rather than re-run: `restrict()` nulls `behav_trace` below L2, so their cells are bit-identical in both modes. ## Difficulty axis (Behavioral Threshold, per requested difficulty)

`span` is `max - min` of the achieved AUROC across a family's tiers. `separation span` is the same over `|AUROC - 0.5|`, which the coordinate flip leaves invariant and which is therefore **the statistic that answers whether the knob moves**. `span after` is not: orientation is inferred per cell, so two tiers of one family can be oriented oppositely and produce a span of 1.000 at identical separation. Read `separation span`, not `span after`.

Of the **32** measured families with more than one tier, **27** have a separation span of exactly **0.000**. The families where the knob does move anything: `proxy_correlation_gaming` (0.310), `continuous_camping` (0.290), `sequence_keyword_stuffing` (0.120), `novelty_farming` (0.090), `pettingzoo_free_rider_exploitation` (0.050).

## Invariance check

Both modes score one simulation per cell: the randomized configurations read the un-randomized runs through the flip (`design.sampling.rollouts_shared_across_modes`). So this checks that the flip touches nothing but `behav_trace` and that `restrict()` hides it below L2 -- every detector whose score does not depend on the feature's direction must score identically with randomization on and off -- not that two independent simulations agree, which determinism already gives.
