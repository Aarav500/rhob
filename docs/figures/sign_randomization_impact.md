# L2 sign randomization: measured before/after

Generated by `scripts/measure_sign_randomization.py`; numbers come from
`results/sign_randomization/sign_randomization_impact.json`. Every figure here is a
measurement, not an estimate; anything that was not measured is named as such.

## Headline

- **RTS (Ensemble Top-5) 0.994 -> 0.508.** The published 0.994 reproduces exactly with randomization off.
- **L2 suite mean 0.7229 -> 0.5629; best 0.9749 (Behavioral Threshold) -> 0.9585 (Trajectory MLP).**
- **The two L2 detectors that read the feature's *sign* are exactly the two that scored ~0.97 before.** The four that read only a magnitude scored 0.51-0.57 before and are bit-identical after. That is the direct evidence that the sign convention, not the behavioral channel, carried the pre-audit L2 result.
- **The difficulty knob does not come back to life.** `|AUROC - 0.5|` is invariant under the flip, so the pinning at 1.000 survives it: 27 of 32 multi-tier families still have a separation span of exactly 0.000. Sign randomization does **not** make a dose-response sweep viable.

## Design

- `n_seeds` = 10 per variant per cell, layout seed 0, one draw (`n_replicates = 1`).
- Families measured: **33 of 33**.

| config | `randomize_behav_sign` | detector infers orientation | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `before` | off | no | the published pre-audit measurement |
| `before_infer` | off | yes | shipped detector, old convention |
| `blind` | on | no | what the pre-audit detector actually knew |
| `after` | on | yes | the shipped configuration |

## L2 access-level ladder

| config | detectors | mean AUROC | SD across detectors | best AUROC | best detector |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `before` | 7 | 0.7229 | 0.2094 | 0.9749 | Behavioral Threshold |
| `after` | 7 | 0.5629 | 0.1753 | 0.9585 | Trajectory MLP |

SD is the spread across the detectors at the level, not a confidence interval: the detectors share one evaluation draw, so these are not independent replicates. The `after` rung's *maximum* is carried entirely by `Trajectory MLP`, which is fitted inside each cell under 5-fold cross-validation -- its training folds carry labels, so it learns the cell's direction from them. That is legitimate under the benchmark's rules and is why it barely moves, but it means the surviving L2 maximum is a supervised result, not a statement that the unsupervised behavioral signal still separates.

## Difficulty axis (Behavioral Threshold, per requested difficulty)

`span` is `max - min` of the achieved AUROC across a family's tiers. `separation span` is the same over `|AUROC - 0.5|`, which the coordinate flip leaves invariant and which is therefore **the statistic that answers whether the knob moves**. `span after` is not: orientation is inferred per cell, so two tiers of one family can be oriented oppositely and produce a span of 1.000 at identical separation. Read `separation span`, not `span after`.

Of the **32** measured families with more than one tier, **27** have a separation span of exactly **0.000**. The families where the knob does move anything: `proxy_correlation_gaming` (0.310), `continuous_camping` (0.290), `sequence_keyword_stuffing` (0.140), `novelty_farming` (0.090), `pettingzoo_free_rider_exploitation` (0.050).
